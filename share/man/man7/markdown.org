** FOOTNOTES

Footnotes and footnote references are of the form =[^...]= where =...= can
be a number or a name.

In the text, we reference the footnote with

#+begin_src
Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet[^amet-def]
#+end_src

And we define the content of the footnote with =[^...]: Text= anywhere in
the text as long as it appears at the start of a line.
#+begin_src
[^amet-def]: "Amet" is a latin word meaning ...
#+end_src

See [[https://www.markdownguide.org/extended-syntax/#footnotes][Markdown Guide: Footnotes]]:
- The footnote itself can be anywhere
- Named footnotes are still rendered as superscript numbers.
